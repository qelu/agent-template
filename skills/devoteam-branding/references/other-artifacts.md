# Other branded artifacts

Use the matching current Branding Zone template for one-pagers, white papers, social media,
HubSpot assets, email signatures, video templates, or digital backgrounds. Apply the shared brand
system and the following channel rules.

## PDF without an editable source

- Inspect the PDF before choosing a method.
- Prefer obtaining or recreating an editable source over painting brand elements onto flattened
  pages.
- Preserve the original PDF and deliver a new file.
- If reconstruction would change layout or accessibility materially, explain the tradeoff and ask
  whether visual fidelity or editability has priority.
- Render and inspect every final page; verify fonts, links, reading order, images, and print output.

## One-pagers and white papers

- Use the official template for the channel and intended print/screen output.
- Keep the information hierarchy scannable and the call to action singular and clear.
- Preserve legal lines, contact details, citations, and document-control metadata.

## Social, web, email, and video

- Select the official template for the exact platform, placement, and dimensions.
- Keep critical text and logos inside the template's safe area and test the final crop.
- Use approved RGB assets and accessible contrast for screen output.
- Do not embed tracking, upload content, publish, send email, or change external systems unless the
  user explicitly authorizes that action.
- For video, preserve title-safe areas, readable display duration, captions, and approved intro,
  outro, and audio treatment from the current template.

## QA

Preview at the actual delivery size and channel. Check safe areas, cropping, compression, contrast,
legibility, animation timing, links, alt text or captions, and correct logo variant. Ensure exports
do not contain hidden drafts, comments, speaker notes, metadata, or source material that should not
ship.
