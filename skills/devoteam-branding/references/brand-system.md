# Devoteam brand-system fallback

This baseline was revalidated against the authenticated Branding Zone on 2026-08-13. Recheck the
current official source before use.

## Color roles

| Role | Color | HEX | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Red Poppy | `#F8485E` | Selected headings, icons, highlights, and calls to action; use sparingly |
| Primary | Dark Grey | `#3C3C3A` | Default dark text; never replace with full black |
| Primary | White | `#FFFFFF` | Active whitespace and text on sufficiently dark backgrounds |
| Secondary | Poppy Light | `#FCA2AE` | Backgrounds and decorative graphics, never text |
| Secondary | Poppy Lighter | `#FDDADE` | Backgrounds and decorative graphics, never text |
| Secondary | Aqua | `#D7EBE7` | Backgrounds and decorative graphics, never text |
| Secondary | Beige | `#EFEADC` | Backgrounds and decorative graphics, never text |
| Secondary | Light Grey | `#EFEEEE` | Backgrounds and decorative graphics, never text |
| Accent | Intense Fire | `#FCC354` | Section coding, charts, data visualization, and infographics |
| Accent | Fresh Mint | `#5AB891` | Section coding, charts, data visualization, and infographics |
| Accent | Blue Lagoon | `#4A8CCA` | Section coding, charts, data visualization, and infographics |
| Accent | Candy | `#EC86A3` | Section coding, charts, data visualization, and infographics |
| Accent | Violet | `#63238C` | Section coding, charts, data visualization, and infographics |

Use primary colors for text, icons, and calls to action. Keep white generous and active. Use
secondary colors only for backgrounds or decorative graphics. Reserve accents for meaningful
coding and data graphics; pair color with labels, patterns, or direct annotation.

## Typography

- Use Montserrat for Devoteam communications.
- Use Montserrat Light for most body copy and Montserrat Regular when small text needs more
  readability.
- Use Montserrat Light or Bold for the tagline: Light at larger sizes, Bold at smaller sizes.
- Place the tagline near the end of copy unless the official template specifies otherwise.
- Use Dark Grey for normal text, White on dark or image backgrounds, and Red Poppy for selected
  highlights and headings.
- Use Aptos as the approved Microsoft Office alternative when Montserrat is unavailable or
  impractical. Do not introduce another typeface without explicit approval.
- Preserve official template-native families, weights, and sizes unless a concrete readability or
  fit defect requires a local repair.

## Logos and badges

- Use RGB assets for screen output and the approved production variant for print.
- Use the normal/dark wordmark on light backgrounds and the approved white variant on dark ones.
- Keep team-name marks internal-only unless current official guidance explicitly says otherwise.
- Distinguish partner logos from partner badges; they are not interchangeable.
- Preserve clear space, contrast, aspect ratio, and intrinsic artwork.
- Never reconstruct, typeset, recolor, crop, distort, outline, shadow, or apply effects to a mark.
