# Presentations

## Construction

1. Copy the complete current official presentation template.
2. Inspect its slide library, layouts, theme, masters, and relevant exemplars before building.
3. Map each planned slide to an exemplar or layout by narrative role, density, evidence type, and
   meaning-bearing slots.
4. Duplicate a rich exemplar when its design depends on slide-local shapes, charts, image frames,
   custom styles, or footer treatment. Use a layout when inherited placeholders are sufficient.
5. Create a new composition only when no official pattern fits; align it with the nearest official
   family.
6. Keep unused library slides until the delivered order and completeness are verified; remove them
   last.

## Preservation

- Preserve theme, master/layout styling, recurring furniture, slide numbers, notes, links, editable
  text, tables, charts, and native media types.
- Preserve mixed run and paragraph styles when replacing text; do not flatten a text box.
- Map every template image, chart, video, portrait, and screenshot slot to keep, replace, or delete.
- Use native bullets and remove empty bullet paragraphs.
- Preserve media aspect ratios and do not crop meaning-bearing content.
- Prefer a better layout or tighter wording over unreadably small text.
- Use accents consistently for data series and direct-label charts where practical.

## QA

Render every delivered slide. Check clipping, overflow, overlap, orphaned labels, stretched media,
stale placeholders, broken charts, empty bullets, contrast, alignment, recurring-role consistency,
and presenter readability. Re-render after any change to slide structure, IDs, count, or order.
